KIRIMSTELLAR 05 / 11 Why Stellar

The right chain for money that moves.

  • Fast & cheap cross-border payments — Stellar's core strength is exactly what remittance needs.
  • Soroban smart contracts enable non-custodial escrow with timelock. No admin key, no middleman risk.
  • Built-in DEX auto-converts USDC → IDRT with no external bridge.
  • Existing stablecoin ecosystem — USDC for transport, IDRT for the last-mile Rupiah off-ramp.
  • 5-second ledger close means money arrives before a coffee gets cold.
